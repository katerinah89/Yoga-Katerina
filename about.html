Meet Katerina

My work is rooted in yoga, breath, self-inquiry, and the belief that what you are searching for is already within you.

I create spaces where you can soften, listen, and reconnect with yourself — gently, honestly, and without pressure.

The Jóga seberozvoje Method

Yoga that goes beyond the mat.

This is not yoga as exercise. This is yoga as a way of knowing yourself.
Through movement, breath, and reflection, the practice becomes a path back to your body, your rhythm, and your own inner guidance. It is a gentle but deeply transformative process that helps you release tension, regulate your nervous system, and return to a more conscious way of living.
  • Reconnect with your body through slow, intelligent movement and somatic awareness.
  • Use the breath as a practical tool for calm, clarity, and emotional balance.
  • Create space for reflection, so daily life starts to feel more honest, grounded, and aligned.

Body

Gentle movement and somatic awareness to release tension and reconnect with your physical self.

Breath

Breathwork practices to regulate the nervous system, soften stress, and restore inner clarity.

Reflection

Guided self-inquiry inspired by yoga philosophy and Ayurveda for more conscious living.

Experience & Benefits

What this practice supports

Not perfection. Not pressure. Just a steadier way of meeting yourself, your body, and your daily life with more awareness, calm, and clarity.

Stress regulation

Support your system in moving out of constant tension and into a calmer, steadier rhythm.

Emotional clarity

Feel what is true beneath the noise, so your choices begin to come from honesty rather than overwhelm.

Nervous system balance

Use breath and embodied awareness to build more resilience, softness, and internal stability.

Better boundaries

Become more attuned to your limits, needs, and energy — and more able to honour them with calm confidence.

Inner grounding

Return to the body as a place of support, helping you feel less scattered and more anchored in yourself.

More conscious daily living

Bring the practice beyond the mat, so presence, awareness, and self-connection start shaping everyday life.
