Why This Practice

Begin the day from calm rather than chaos.

Most of us begin the day in reaction. The alarm goes. The phone lights up. The day begins before we have properly arrived in it.

This practice is designed to interrupt that pattern with something very simple: five minutes of conscious breathing before you begin.

It does not require a yoga mat, a particular belief system, or any previous experience. It requires only five minutes and the willingness to pay attention — to your breath, your body, and how you actually feel.

Over time, this single habit creates a very different relationship with the rest of the day.

The most powerful practices are not the most complicated ones. They are the ones you actually do.
— Katerina
