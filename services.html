Location and Practical Information

Simple, clear, and easy to prepare for.

In-person Sessions

Sessions take place at a dedicated yoga space in Prague — the full address is shared upon booking. The space is easily accessible by metro (Line A) and has public parking nearby.

Online Sessions

Online sessions take place via Zoom. A link is sent 24 hours before each session. You need only a stable internet connection, a quiet space, and a mat if you have one.

Session Preparation

Please wear comfortable clothing you can move in. For online sessions, arrange a space where you can lie down and will not be interrupted. No yoga experience is required — ever.
