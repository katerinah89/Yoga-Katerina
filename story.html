Founder Story

My Story

Yoga instructor focusing on self-growth (inner work), stress management, and conscious bodywork

I am a yoga teacher and guide who helps people find inner peace, balance, and a firm foothold in a hectic world. I focus on working with the body, breath, and mind so that everyone can slow down, better understand themselves, and find more joy in life.

In my work, I combine traditional yoga with conscious breathing and self-discovery techniques that support your ability to see life from new angles and find your own path to harmony. I offer individual lessons and mentoring, where we look together for what really helps you feel better and live a more fulfilling life.

My approach is always respectful and tailored to your needs and pace. I am here to support you on your journey to greater lightness, resilience and inner stability.
